## Featured project: Hooty

[**Hooty — Safety Intelligence MVP**](https://github.com/stanfordgibson/hooty-showcase)

An address-to-report prototype combining historical crime data, geospatial analysis, deterministic safety assessments and a Gemini conversation layer. The AI explains the supplied report without changing its calculated risk level.

The public showcase includes screenshots from the working demo, a video, architecture, engineering decisions and an honest account of remaining work. Application source remains private.
